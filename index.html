Three rounds. Ninety-nine seconds each.

Move freely in any direction relative to the camera, like in Genshin Impact. Soft targeting assists your aim and lock-on keeps the camera on your opponent. Win two rounds and the arena is yours.

Knockout

Bring your opponent’s health to 0.

Ring-out

Knock them off the edge and into the lava.

Time out

When the clock hits zero, whoever has more health wins the round.

Learn the controls

Light attacks beat nothing, heavy attacks beat blocks, grabs beat blocks too. Stamina is unlimited, so keep moving.

Attack

Left click3-hit light combo
Right clickHeavy attack that breaks a block
FGrab that beats a block
JumpAttack while airborne

Defend

Left CtrlHold to block, tap just before a hit to parry
ShiftDodge, or air dash in the air

Magic and healing

Q E RSkills and ultimate, 100 to 300 magic
M+30 magic every 5 seconds
ZHeal 5% of your health every 20 seconds

Three ways to play

Training

Practice against a bot with optional unlimited magic and a hitbox display.

Versus bot

A full best-of-3 match against the computer.

Online

The host shares a room code and a friend joins with it. Rollback netcode hides lag, and a green ping display sits in the top left.
